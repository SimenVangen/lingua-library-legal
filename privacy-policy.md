# Lingua Library Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

Lingua Library is built to need as little of your data as possible. Here's exactly what the app stores and why.

## What stays on your device

Your reading progress (which books you've started or finished, and your current page), your saved Practice words, and your reminder preference are all stored locally on your device using standard iOS on-device storage. None of it is sent to us or to any server we run — there isn't one. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Subscriptions and payment

Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your card details. Subscription status (active, trialing, expired) is managed by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider, which receives an anonymous device-generated identifier and your purchase/transaction data from Apple in order to tell the app whether you're entitled to the library. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on their handling of that data.

## Notifications

The optional daily reading reminder is a local notification scheduled entirely on your device — it does not involve a push notification server, and no data about your usage is transmitted anywhere to make it work.

## No accounts, no ads

Lingua Library does not require creating an account, does not show ads, and does not include third-party analytics or ad-tracking SDKs.

## Children

Lingua Library is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to wordup.support1@gmail.com, or from the Help & Feedback screen in the app.
