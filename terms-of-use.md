## Subscriptions

Lingua Library's library of books is available through an auto-renewing subscription, offered as a monthly plan and an annual plan. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current billing period ends, your subscription renews automatically and payment is charged to your Apple ID account at the price shown at purchase.

## Managing and cancelling

You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals but does not refund the current billing period. If you cancel during the free trial, you will not be charged.

## Free trial eligibility

The 7-day free trial is available once per Apple ID / App Store account. If you have already used a trial for Lingua Library, a new subscription starts at full price.

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple, not by us, since all payment is processed through the App Store. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
