# Lingua Library Support

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback? Email us at wordup.support1@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Go to your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lingua Library → Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage subscription" inside the app's More tab, which takes you to the same place.

**Will I be charged during the free trial?**
No. If you cancel any time before the 7-day trial ends, you won't be charged.

**Does the free trial work more than once?**
The free trial is available once per Apple ID. Subscribing again after a prior trial starts at full price.

**My purchase didn't unlock the library — what do I do?**
Open the app, go to More → Manage subscription, and tap "Restore purchases." If that doesn't resolve it, email wordup.support1@gmail.com with your Apple ID's email (not your password) and we'll help sort it out.
